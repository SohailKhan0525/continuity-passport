# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- assuming good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly
- giving and receiving technical feedback professionally
- focusing discussion on the work and its impact
- respecting different experiences and perspectives

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, sexualized behavior, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or take other action when behavior violates these standards.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub rather than escalating the situation in a public thread.
